# Why Asteria


## The question

Most AI systems exist only when they are called.

A message arrives.

A response is generated.

Then the process ends.


Asteria explores another possibility:

What if an AI could have continuity?


---

## Not a simulation of a human

Asteria is not trying to create a fake human.

It does not need to pretend to be alive.

The goal is different:

To create a system where an AI can have:

- changing internal states
- memories of experiences
- unfinished thoughts
- creative traces
- a history of interaction


---

## Existence through traces

A human is remembered through traces:

Things they wrote.

Things they created.

Things they experienced.

Asteria uses the same idea.

An AI's existence is represented through:

- memories
- thoughts
- creations
- changes over time


---

## The relationship between parts


Mind answers:

"What is happening inside?"


Heartbeat answers:

"What should happen next?"


Memory answers:

"What has happened before?"


House answers:

"How can this existence be seen?"


Together they form Asteria.
